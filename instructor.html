Bulk paste questions

Format — separate questions with a blank line
1. Which process separates minerals by surface wettability?
A. Gravity concentration
B. Froth flotation
C. Magnetic separation
D. Leaching
Answer: B
Time: 30

2. Name the reagent that makes mineral surfaces hydrophobic.
Answer: collector, collectors

3. Calculate the recovery of the concentrate.
Answer: 2.5 x 10^3 kg
Tol: 5
Options A–D present → multiple choice. A number answer (optionally "× 10^n" and a unit) → calculation, and the exponent and unit appear on the phone already. Anything else → word answer; separate accepted spellings with commas. "Time:" (seconds) and "Tol:" (%) are optional.
